MEET THE BREED

Beagle

The Beagle is a breed of small scent hound, similar in appearance to the much larger foxhound. The Beagle was developed primarily for hunting hare, known as beagling. With a great sense of smell and tracking instinct, the Beagle is employed as a detection dog for prohibited agricultural imports and foodstuffs in quarantine around the world.

DID YOU KNOW?

Beagle Trivia

  1. Beagles have an excellent sense of smell and are often used as detection dogs.
  2. They are friendly, curious, and generally good with children.
  3. Beagles were originally bred to hunt rabbits and hares.
  4. Their long, floppy ears help collect scent particles near the nose.
  5. A Beagle's tail often has a white tip, which helps hunters spot the dog in tall grass.
